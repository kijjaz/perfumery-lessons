## 🎯 Lab Objective
Develop a professional-grade smelling technique, evaluate physical evaporation on smelling strips, and catalog detailed olfactory profiles for **Linalool (10%)**, **Linalyl Acetate (10%)**, **Sweet Orange (10%)**, and **Bergamot (10%)**.

---

## 🥽 Lab Safety & Preparation Rules

1. Work in a clean room with neutral air (no scented candles, food odors, or active ventilation currents).
2. Wash your hands with scent-free soap before starting.
3. Keep neat raw materials closed; use only the prepared 10% dilutions in ethanol.
4. Keep smelling strips at least 2 inches away from your nose to avoid touching liquid to your skin or nasal passage.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Lab Protocol

```text
  [Step 1: Prep Strip] ➔ [Step 2: Dip 5mm] ➔ [Step 3: Label Strip] ➔ [Step 4: Smell & Log]
  (Fold down spine)     (Avoid touching sides)   (Write name & time)    (Smell skin to reset)
```

1. **Prepare Smelling Strips:** Fold a smelling strip along its center spine to stiffen the paper. Write the material name (e.g., "Linalool 10%") on the wide end of the strip.
2. **Execute the Dip:** Dip the narrow end of the strip exactly **5mm** into the bottle containing the dilution. Do not touch the sides of the bottle mouth.
3. **The First Sniff (Alcohol Flashoff):** Wait exactly **30 seconds** for the volatile ethanol solvent to evaporate (flash off) before smelling.
4. **Olfactory Evaluation Method:**
   * Hold the strip 2 inches below your nose.
   * Take 3 short, gentle inhales (bunny-sniffs) rather than one deep breath.
   * Close your eyes and visualize the scent profile. Focus on the vocabulary descriptors.
   * Write your immediate observations in the evaluation logs below.
5. **Olfactory Bulb Reset:** Rest for 60 seconds before moving to the next strip. Smell the sleeve of your clean cotton shirt or crook of your arm to reset your receptors.
