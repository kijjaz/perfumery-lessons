## 🎯 Lab Objective
Draft a balanced 4-part formula containing exactly one material for each functional role in the Architectural Casting System. Input this formula into the *Student Organ* app, analyze its volatility spread, and use the *3D Molecular Space* engine to verify its physical structural distribution.

---

## 🥽 Lab Safety & Preparation Rules
1. Calibrate your scale and ensure your workplace is clean and free of drafts.
2. Formulate in a clean, 10ml amber glass vial.
3. Keep all bottles closed when not actively transferring liquids.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Lab Protocol

```text
  [Identify Roles] ➔ [Draft Formula] ➔ [Blend & Weigh] ➔ [Enter in Student Organ] ➔ [Analyze 3D Space]
```

1. **Cast Your Materials:** Select one top-note Modifier, one Blender, one Heart note, and one Base-note Fixative from your organ.
2. **Draft the Formula:** Design a 1.000g formula using a balanced ratio (e.g., 30% Modifier, 20% Blender, 30% Heart, 20% Fixative).
3. **Compound the Accord:** Weigh and drop each ingredient into your vial, recording the exact weights to three decimal places. Swirl to mix.
4. **App Integration:** Open the *Perfumery Student Organ* app. Enter your formula, inputting the CAS numbers and weights of your materials.
5. **3D Topology Check:** Load the *3D Molecular Space* visualizer. Observe the spatial clustering of your molecules.
