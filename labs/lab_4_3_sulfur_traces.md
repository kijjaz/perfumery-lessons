## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Lab Protocol

### 🌀 Part 1: Serial Dilution of the Sulfur Compound
Because sulfur compounds are active at ppt levels, you must dilute them to a workable strength. We will prepare a **0.01% w/w solution** from a standard 1% stock.

```text
  [ 1% Stock Solution ] ➔ (Add 9.00g DPG to 1.00g stock) ➔ [ 0.1% Dilution ] 
                                                               │
                                                               ▼ (Add 9.00g DPG to 1.00g of 0.1%)
                                                           [ 0.01% Dilution (100 ppm) ]
```

1. **Prepare 0.1% Dilution:**
   * Place a clean 10ml amber bottle on the scale. Tare it to 0.000g.
   * Add exactly **1.000g** of your **1% stock solution** of **cis-Galbanum Oxathiane (Oxane)** (or Buchu Mercaptan).
   * Add exactly **9.000g** of **DPG solvent**.
   * Cap immediately and shake vigorously for 60 seconds. Label this bottle: `[OXANE 0.1% in DPG]`.
2. **Prepare 0.01% Dilution (100 ppm):**
   * Place a second clean 10ml amber bottle on the scale. Tare it to 0.000g.
   * Add exactly **1.000g** of your newly prepared **0.1% solution**.
   * Add exactly **9.000g** of **DPG solvent**.
   * Cap immediately and shake vigorously for 60 seconds. Label this bottle: `[OXANE 0.01% in DPG]`.
   * **Deactivate:** Submerge the pipette used in step 1 and step 2 into the bleach bath.
