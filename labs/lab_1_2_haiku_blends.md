## 🎯 Lab Objective
Apply Jean-Claude Ellena’s philosophy of olfactory minimalism by constructing three high-impact "Olfactory Haikus" using exactly two raw materials in each blend. Analyze how the interaction between synthetic molecules creates realistic, complex natural illusions.

---

## 🥽 Lab Safety & Preparation Rules
1. Work at your designated station with your analytical milligram scale calibrated and tared.
2. Use clean glass pipettes for each transfer to prevent cross-contaminating your 10% dilutions.
3. Formulate in clean, 10ml amber glass vials.
4. Record all measurements to three decimal places (**0.001g**).

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Lab Protocol

```text
  [Tare Empty Vial] ➔ [Weigh Component A] ➔ [Record Weight] ➔ [Weigh Component B] ➔ [Record Weight & Blend]
```

1. **Vial Placement:** Place a clean, empty 10ml amber glass vial onto your scale and press the **TARE** button. Verify the reading is exactly `0.000g`.
2. **Add Component A:** Using a clean pipette, drop the target amount of the first material (Component A) into the vial. Record the exact weight in your log sheet.
3. **Add Component B:** Using a fresh pipette, drop the target amount of the second material (Component B) into the vial. Record the final weight.
4. **Mix the Blend:** Cap the vial securely and gently swirl the liquid for 30 seconds to ensure the molecules mix thoroughly.
5. **Evaluate:** Dip a smelling strip 5mm into the blend, wait 30 seconds for the alcohol to flash off, and evaluate using the "bunny sniff" technique.
