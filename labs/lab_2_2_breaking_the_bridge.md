## 🎯 Lab Objective
Witness composition fracturing firsthand by compounding two variations of a floral-woody accord: Accord A (Bridged) containing a monoterpene alcohol blender, and Accord B (Fractured) where the blender is systematically removed and replaced with solvent. Evaluate the two blends side-by-side over a 2-hour evaporation curve.

---

## 🥽 Lab Safety & Preparation Rules
1. Work at your station with your scale calibrated and tared.
2. Label two clean 10ml amber vials: "Accord A (Bridged)" and "Accord B (Fractured)."
3. Use SDA-40B Perfumer's Alcohol (or DPG if alcohol is unavailable) to balance the formulas.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Lab Protocol

```text
  [Compound Accord A] ➔ [Compound Accord B (No Bridge)] ➔ [Dip & Label Two Strips] ➔ [Smell & Compare]
```

1. **Compound Accord A (Bridged):** Tare your first vial. Add the ingredients listed under the Accord A formulation table below. Record the actual weights. Swirl to mix.
2. **Compound Accord B (Fractured):** Tare your second vial. Add the ingredients listed under the Accord B table. Note that Linalool is replaced entirely with pure solvent to keep dilution percentages identical. Swirl to mix.
3. **Smelling Strip Preparation:** Dip one strip 5mm into Accord A and another strip 5mm into Accord B. Label the strips clearly.
4. **Olfactory Comparison Schedule:** Smell and compare the two strips at the following intervals:
   * **1 Minute** (Initial impression after solvent flashoff)
   * **15 Minutes** (Transition phase)
   * **45 Minutes** (Heart phase)
   * **90 Minutes** (Base phase)
