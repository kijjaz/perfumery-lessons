Level 4 — Bottle #4

Ethylene Brassylate (Astratone / CAS 105-95-3)

The smooth macrocyclic musk cushion that suspends dense balsamic resins in weightless velvet, providing warm skin radiance, subtle berry powder, and sublime diffusion.

Why Compose with Ethylene Brassylate?

1. The 3 Eras of Musk

• Nitro-Musks (Musk Ambrette, Xylene): Banned due to neurotoxicity/phototoxicity.
• Polycyclic Musks (Galaxolide): Highly persistent, bioaccumulating in aquatic life.
• Macrocyclic Musks (Ethylene Brassylate): Completely skin-safe, biodegradable, and mimics natural ambrette seed oil!

2. Overcoming Musk Anosmia

Large musk molecules have high molecular weights (~270 g/mol). Many humans are anosmic (scent-blind) to individual musks. Ethylene Brassylate is a "bridge musk"—soft, slightly sweet, and easily perceived by over 95% of human noses.

3. The Acoustic Cushion for Resins

Heavy resins (Benzoin, Labdanum, Vanilla) have low volatility and can sit flat and heavy against skin like thick paste. Ethylene Brassylate acts as a spring cushion, lifting heavy resins into an airy, diffusive scent cloud.

4. Zero IFRA Restriction

Ethylene Brassylate has no IFRA Category 4 restriction ceiling. Perfumers routinely dose it at 5%, 10%, or 20%+ to create luxurious "second-skin" sillage without legal limits.

The Multi-Facet Reality of Ethylene Brassylate

Never treat musk as just "invisible fixative". It has a distinct sensorial texture:

Facet 1 (Dominant)
Warm Intimate Skin

Clean, sun-warmed human skin after a warm bath, comforting and intimate.

Facet 2 (Fruity)
Powdery Blackberry & Red Currant

A delicate sweet red berry and ambrette seed undertone that softens florals.

Facet 3 (Gourmand)
Sweet Vanilla Pastry Sheen

A subtle lactonic, creamy sweetness that locks directly into vanillic drydowns.

Facet 4 (Tactile)
Cashmere & White Angora Fur

Tactile velvet softness that makes sharp alcohol transitions feel seamless.

1 What is this sensation?

Dip a smelling strip into your **Ethylene Brassylate 10% solution**.

At first sniff, you may think: "Wait, this is so quiet, is anything there?" Do not push your nose right into the paper—wave the strip 10 cm away. Suddenly, you will catch a warm, velvety, sweet cashmere veil floating in the room. It feels like slipping on a newly washed, luxurious white sweater.

Organ Connections & Structural Bridges

  • Bridge to Ethyl Vanillin (Level 4): Ethylene Brassylate carries a sweet vanillic facet that blooms when paired with Ethyl Vanillin, preventing vanilla from smelling like flat baking extract.
  • Bridge to Rose / PEA (Level 1): Musks expand floral notes into 3D space, softening the honeyed sharpness of Phenylethyl alcohol into dewy cashmere rose petals.
  • Bridge to Labdanum & Benzoin (Level 3 & 4): In *Shalimar*, macrocyclic musks suspend heavy balsams, creating that unforgettable trail of sillage that lingers in rooms long after the wearer has departed.
