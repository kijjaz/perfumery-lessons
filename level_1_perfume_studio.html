3 Stage 5: Testing & Blotter Diagnostics

Before committing your concentrate to alcohol, evaluate the core interaction on smelling strips over time:

0 to 15 Minutes (Opening)

Italian Bergamot FCF explodes with sparkling zest, cut subtly by 2% green leaf sap to prevent candy sweetness.

30 Min to 2 Hours (Heart)

Rose (PEA) blooms gently as the citrus calms. The floral note is dry and clean, supported by the transparent cedar skeleton.

4 to 8+ Hours (Drydown)

Iso E Super and Vanillin 10% fuse on skin into a warm, comforting cashmere halo that lingers intimate and clean.
