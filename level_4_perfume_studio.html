Terminology Evolution: Why We Say "Amber", Not "Oriental"

For decades, 20th-century European fragrance literature referred to this family as "Oriental." In 2021, the global fragrance industry (led by the Fragrance Foundation and master perfumers) formally abandoned this term.

Why the shift? Beyond retiring an outdated colonial stereotype, the term was scientifically vague. "Amber" is chemically precise: it refers directly to the historic accord of Ambre 83—the golden synergy of Labdanum resin + Benzoin balsam + Vanillin/Ethyl Vanillin. Calling this family Amber roots our craft in real raw materials, resin chemistry, and tactile balsamic warmth.
