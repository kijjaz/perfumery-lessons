What Does Lavender Bring to the Table?

1. The Clean Barbershop Spark

Level 1 had sweetness and rose, but zero bracing herbal freshness. Lavender introduces camphoraceous cleanliness and crisp aromatic sophistication.

2. The Secret Bridge to Citrus

Natural lavender is 50% Linalyl Acetate (like bergamot!) and 30% Linalool. It seamlessly melds with Level 1's citrus and carries it deep into the heart.

3. The Fougère Engine Key

Without lavender, you cannot make the legendary 1882 Fougère chassis. It is the vital Pillar 1 that counterbalances warm sweet coumarin.

4. Natural 200-Molecule Symphony

High-altitude French lavender (*Lavandula angustifolia*) contains trace ocimenes and borneol, giving it an authentic sunlit herbal texture impossible to mimic with single synthetics alone.

The Modern Revolution: Replacing Lavender with DHM & Dimetol

In traditional 19th-century formulas, natural Lavender was irreplaceable. But in the 1980s, a seismic shift occurred in fine perfumery. Perfumers realized they could replace part or all of natural Lavender with two synthetic molecules: Dihydromyrcenol (DHM) and Dimetol.

1. Stripping the "Old-Fashioned" Baggage

Natural lavender contains organic camphor, borneol, and herbal hay facets. To younger consumers, this often smells like mothballs, antique medicinal salves, or grandmother's linen drawer.

2. Isolating the "Electric Citrus-Lavender" Core

Dihydromyrcenol (DHM) isolates the sparkling lime-lavender ester facet, but makes it metallic, radiant, and hyper-clean. Dimetol provides a softer, smoother, ultra-fresh citrus-lavender aura without any medicinal bite.

3. The 1982 Milestone: Drakkar Noir

Pierre Wargnye took the classical Fougère chassis and injected a shocking 10% Dihydromyrcenol overdose into Drakkar Noir (1982). It redefined modern masculine grooming into an electric, showered-clean powerhouse.

4. The 1988 Culmination: Cool Water

Pierre Bourdon replaced even more natural lavender with DHM and Dimetol in Davidoff Cool Water (1988). Combined with Calone, it transformed the vintage barbershop into the first oceanic marine wave!

Synthetic Powerhouse
Dihydromyrcenol (DHM)

Facets: Metallic Lime + Electric Lavender + Shower-Gel Clean.
Zeros out all medicinal dust; creates radiant, athletic, ultra-modern projection.

Smooth Modern Elegance
Dimetol

Facets: Soft Lemony Lavender + Transparent Floral Green.
Smoother and less metallic than DHM. Blends effortlessly into both masculine and feminine modern hearts.
