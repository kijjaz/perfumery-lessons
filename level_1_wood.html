Woods & Roots (The Living Forest)

The dry, quiet texture of ancient tree trunks and damp roots that clings to skin for days.

Why Compose Wood Notes from Raw Materials?

1. The Weight & Mud Trap of 100% Naturals

Pure natural woods (Cedarwood Virginia, Sandalwood, Vetiver) are thick, viscous, and heavy. If you only use natural oils, the perfume feels "muddy" and sits flat like oily wood varnish without air.

2. Transparent Modern Physics (Iso E Super)

Synthetics like Iso E Super and Cedrol provide the feeling of dry cedarwood without the oily sludge. They allow light to pass through the perfume like clean window glass.

3. Tailoring Your Forest Atmosphere

Do you want an aristocratic Scandinavian sauna (high Iso E Super + Atlas Cedar) or a damp jungle temple floor (Vetiver + Patchouli)? By controlling the raw components, you set the climate.

4. Living Root Organic Texture

A synthetic wood alone can smell like a dry cardboard box. Adding a touch of natural Patchouli Oil or Vetiver instantly adds the damp forest soil, roots, and fungal life that make wood smell alive.

1 How to Build a Living Forest Wood Note (100.0 Parts Total)

A living tree note is a dialogue between transparent synthetic fiber and natural root depth (calibrated out of 100.0 Parts):

1. The Core Wood Trunk (Synthetic Monolith): Iso E Super — 65.0 parts (0.65 g)
Velvety cedar pencil shavings that expand around the wearer like an invisible cashmere sweater.
2. The Resinous Bark (Natural Depth): Natural Cedarwood Atlas or Texas — 25.0 parts (0.25 g)
Adds the authentic, resinous, slightly smoky ring of real tree bark.
3. The Forest Soil & Roots (Natural Anchor): Natural Patchouli Oil (Fractionated) — 10.0 parts (0.10 g)
Damp earth, autumn leaves, and mossy mycorrhizal fungi under the roots.

The Multi-Facet Reality of Iso E Super

Never treat Iso E Super as just "pencil wood". It is an optical illusion with multiple hidden facets:

Facet 1 (Dominant)
Dry Cedarwood Shavings

Crisp, clean, tactile pencil shavings and dry sauna wood.

Facet 2 (The Skin Magic)
Velvety Ambergris / Skin

Subtle warmth that smells like warm clean human skin and cozy cashmere.

Facet 3 (Acoustic)
Acoustic Amplifier / Lift

Carries floral and citrus notes upward, giving the perfume vertical projection.

Facet 4 (Illusion)
The Vanishing Mirage

Causes rapid olfactory habituation—you stop smelling it, but others smell you from 6 feet away!

2 What does it do on the bench?

Dip a paper strip into your wood bottle.

  • At 0 minutes: Soft, faint, tactile cedar velvet. You might even wonder if it's there.
  • At 2 hours: It is still quietly humming along, completely unchanged.
  • Tomorrow morning: The strip will still smell like pure cedarwood.

This is the **Fixative Anchor** of perfumery—the heavy floor that catches everything else.
