Citrus & Peels (The Radiant Bergamot)

The electric flash of cold morning light that arrives instantly and vanishes fast.

Why Compose Citrus from Raw Materials?

1. The 15-Minute Evaporation Trap

Pure natural citrus oils (Lemon, Orange) consist of over 90% Limonene. They flash off the skin in 15 minutes. By composing citrus with synthetic esters and floral bridges, you extend the fresh sensation for hours.

2. Phototoxicity & The FCF Mandate

Cold-pressed natural citrus contains bergapten (furocoumarins), which causes severe chemical skin burns and blistering in the sun. Perfumers must understand how to use Bergamot FCF (Furocoumarin-Free) and synthetic esters safely.

3. The Natural Sparkle vs. Synthetic Lift

Natural cold-pressed oils give the juicy, mouth-watering bitter rind; clean synthetic esters like Linalyl Acetate give aerodynamic projection without turning sour or oxidizing into furniture polish on skin.

4. Tailoring Your Citrus Character

By controlling the raw materials, you can decide whether your citrus smells like a sunny Italian afternoon (Bergamot FCF + Neroli) or an electric modern high-tech lime (Dihydromyrcenol).

1 How to Build a Luminous Bergamot Note

A long-lasting, safe citrus note is built by pairing a natural essence with an anchoring ester and floral bridge (calibrated out of 100.0 Parts Total):

1. The Sparkling Ester (Top Flash): Linalyl Acetate — 45.0 parts (0.45 g)
Crisp, clean, mouth-watering bergamot rind without any sticky citrus terpenes.
2. The Living Citrus Soul (Natural Safe Essence): Natural Bergamot Oil FCF — 35.0 parts (0.35 g)
100% phototoxic-safe distilled bergamot. Adds bitter rind juice and sunny Italian micro-texture.
3. The Floral-Woody Parachute (Bridge): Linalool — 20.0 parts (0.20 g)
The floral-woody alcohol that catches the fast citrus and anchors it into the heart of the perfume.

The Multi-Facet Reality of Bergamot FCF

Never treat Bergamot as just a single "citrus" note. It is a multi-dimensional crystal:

Facet 1 (Dominant)
Zesty Rind Spark

Crisp, mouth-watering bitter citrus oil that pierces the air.

Facet 2 (Hidden)
Lavender-Floral Body

Rich in Linalyl Acetate & Linalool—shares 80% chemistry with Lavender!

Facet 3 (Aromatic)
Earl Grey Tea Tannin

The dry, sophisticated, noble tea flavor known worldwide.

Facet 4 (Modifier)
Peppery Herbal Green

Trace pinene and terpinene give it a crisp botanical needle snap.

2 What does it do on the bench?

Dip a paper strip into your citrus bottle. Set a timer:

  • At 0 minutes: It screams with intense, zesty brightness.
  • At 20 minutes: It has already lost more than half its strength.
  • At 1 hour: It is almost a ghost.

This is why a citrus scent cannot live by itself. It needs friends to catch it when it falls!
