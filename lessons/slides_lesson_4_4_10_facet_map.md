### Slide 2: Facet 1 — Epicarp / Skin (Outer Exocarp & Flavedo Wax)
* **Category:** Spatial / Anatomical
* **Anatomical Origin:** Outer Exocarp & Citrus Flavedo Wax
* **Key Sensory Contribution:** Crisp skin friction, waxy peel bite, crunchy top-note spark
* **Primary Actors:**
  * Hexanal (CAS 66-25-1) – VP 1200 Pa – Sharp C6 green top note
  * (E)-2-Hexenal (CAS 6728-26-3) – VP 480 Pa – Crunchy apple peel skin
  * d-Limonene (CAS 5989-27-5) – VP 190 Pa – Citrus flavedo terpenes
* **Modifiers & Fixatives:** Triplal, Myrac Aldehyde, Cold-Pressed Citrus Oils, Sinensal
* **Olfactory Sensation:** The physical sensation of biting into a taut green apple peel or scratching an orange rind.

---

### Slide 3: Facet 2 — Mesocarp / Pith (Inner Albedo & Cellulosic Walls)
* **Category:** Spatial / Anatomical
* **Anatomical Origin:** Inner Albedo & Cellulosic Parenchyma Walls
* **Key Sensory Contribution:** Dry green brake, bitter pithy astringency, structural white albedo
* **Primary Actors:**
  * Isobutyl Methoxypyrazine (IBMP) (CAS 24683-00-9) – Threshold 0.002 ppb – Hyper-potent green pith pyrazine
  * 1-Hexanol (CAS 111-27-3) – VP 124 Pa – Oily cellulosic green alcohol
* **Modifiers & Fixatives:** Stemone, Rhubofix, Guaiacol, Oakmoss Absolute / Veramoss
* **Olfactory Sensation:** The astringent, dry, white inner pith of a grapefruit or rhubarb stalk; acts as a structural anchor that prevents synthetic candy flattest.

---

### Slide 4: Facet 3 — Fleshy Pulp (Core Parenchyma & Aril Juice)
* **Category:** Spatial / Anatomical
* **Anatomical Origin:** Core Parenchyma & Juice Sac Cells
* **Key Sensory Contribution:** Dominant estery/sweet core identity, succulent mouth-watering flesh
* **Primary Actors:**
  * Ethyl Butanoate (CAS 105-54-4) – VP 1700 Pa – Ultra-diffusive juicy fruity top note
  * Ethyl 2-Methylbutanoate (CAS 7452-79-1) – VP 320 Pa – Ripe apple/berry pulp driver
  * $\gamma$-Decalactone (CAS 706-14-9) – VP 0.12 Pa – Velvety peach/apricot flesh
  * Frambinone (CAS 5471-51-2) – Raspberry pulp ketone
* **Modifiers & Fixatives:** Allyl Caproate, Pear Ester, Hedione, Ambrettolide
* **Olfactory Sensation:** Biting into the sweet, dripping heart of a perfectly ripe peach or passionfruit.

---

### Slide 5: Facet 4 — Aqueous Vacuole (Cytoplasmic Water & Vacuole Sap)
* **Category:** Spatial / Anatomical
* **Anatomical Origin:** Cytoplasmic Water & Vacuole Cell Sap
* **Key Sensory Contribution:** Dewy humidity, watery sillage, aquatic transparency
* **Primary Actors:**
  * cis-3-Hexenol (CAS 928-96-1) – VP 200 Pa – Fresh green dew water
  * Melonal (CAS 106-72-9) – VP 140 Pa – Watery melon juice
  * Calone 1951 (CAS 28940-11-6) – VP 0.05 Pa – Marine-watery humidity
* **Modifiers & Fixatives:** Floralozone, Acetaldehyde, Helional, Florhydral
* **Olfactory Sensation:** The humid, cool spray cloud that bursts into the air when slicing open a fresh melon.

---

### Slide 6: Facet 5 — Mucilage / Aril (Gelatinous Seed Casing Envelope)
* **Category:** Spatial / Anatomical
* **Anatomical Origin:** Gelatinous Seed Casing & Tropical Aril Envelope
* **Key Sensory Contribution:** Slippery lactonic texture, exotic sulfurous veil, creamy aril coating
* **Primary Actors:**
  * 3-Methyl-3-sulfanylbutyl acetate (3M3SBA) (CAS 5287-43-4) – Threshold 0.05 ppb – Mangosteen & durian aril thiol
  * $\delta$-Decalactone (CAS 705-86-2) – VP 0.08 Pa – Creamy delta-lactone membrane
* **Modifiers & Fixatives:** Veloutone, Sulfurol, Ethylene Brassylate, Lactoscatone
* **Olfactory Sensation:** The velvety, slippery coating around mangosteen or passionfruit seeds with trace sulfur exoticism.

---

### Slide 7: Facet 6 — Latex / Sap (Pedicel Stem Bleed & Shrub Resin)
* **Category:** Spatial / Anatomical
* **Anatomical Origin:** Pedicel Stem Bleed & Shrub Resin Ducts
* **Key Sensory Contribution:** Rubbery green sap, fig leaf milk, bitter stem bleed resin
* **Primary Actors:**
  * Stemone (CAS 22457-23-4) – VP 28 Pa – Fig leaf milk and papaya sap oxime
  * Galbanum Oil / Undecavertol (CAS 13351-61-6) – Bitter rubbery sap
* **Modifiers & Fixatives:** Allyl Amyl Glycolate, cis-3-Hexenyl Salicylate, Caryophyllene Oxide
* **Olfactory Sensation:** The milky, bitter, raw sap that bleeds when snapping a green fig stem or unpeeling raw papaya.

### Slide 8: Facet 7 — Endocarp / Seed (Lignified Seed Core & Kernel Oil)
* **Category:** Spatial / Anatomical
* **Anatomical Origin:** Lignified Seed Core, Pit Stone & Kernel Oil
* **Key Sensory Contribution:** Cyanic bitter almond, woody nuttiness, roasted kernel warmth
* **Primary Actors:**
  * Benzaldehyde (CAS 100-52-7) – VP 127 Pa – Cyanic bitter almond / cherry pit core
  * 2,3,5-Trimethylpyrazine (TMP) (CAS 14667-55-1) – VP 110 Pa – Roasted nutty seed core
* **Modifiers & Fixatives:** Eugenol, Cinnamaldehyde, Vanillin, Benzoin Resinoid
* **Olfactory Sensation:** The rich, cyanic, woody bitterness inside a cracked cherry pit or peach stone.

### Slide 9: Facet 8 — Calyx / Stem (Leaf Sepals & Peduncle Crown)
* **Category:** Spatial / Anatomical
* **Anatomical Origin:** Leaf Sepals & Peduncle Crown Base
* **Key Sensory Contribution:** Raw crushed leaf greenness, stem foliage, earthy crown base
* **Primary Actors:**
  * cis-3-Hexenyl Acetate (CAS 3681-71-8) – VP 180 Pa – Sharp green banana leaf and peduncle stem
  * Liffarome (CAS 67633-96-9) – Fresh violet-leaf stem
* **Modifiers & Fixatives:** Triplal, 1-Octen-3-ol, Violet Leaf Absolute, Isobutyl Quinoline
* **Olfactory Sensation:** The crisp, raw green leaves sitting on top of a strawberry or green peduncle stem.

### Slide 10: Facet 9 — Fermentative State (Yeast Glycolysis & Overripe Lees)
* **Category:** Temporal / Thermal Transformation
* **Anatomical Origin:** Yeast Glycolysis & Overripe Lees Transformation
* **Key Sensory Contribution:** Boozy alcoholic lift, rummy wine lees, overripe fruit cider warmth
* **Primary Actors:**
  * Ethyl Acetate (CAS 141-78-6) – VP 9700 Pa – Volatile boozy lift
  * Ethyl Lactate (CAS 97-64-3) – Buttery wine lees and cider
  * 2-Phenylethyl Acetate (CAS 103-45-7) – Honeyed wine ester
* **Modifiers & Fixatives:** Isoamyl Alcohol, Davana Essential Oil, Green Cognac Oil
* **Olfactory Sensation:** The intoxicating, rummy, warm alcoholic sparkle of fermenting plum lees or vintage cider.

### Slide 11: Facet 10 — Desiccated State (Maillard Caramel & Dried Paste)
* **Category:** Temporal / Thermal Transformation
* **Anatomical Origin:** Maillard Reaction & Dehydrated Fruit Paste
* **Key Sensory Contribution:** Jammy cooked syrup, burnt caramel sugar, Hoshigaki dried persimmon
* **Primary Actors:**
  * $\beta$-Damascenone (CAS 23696-85-7) – Threshold 0.002 ppb – Cooked apple & baked plum driver
  * Furaneol / DMHF (CAS 3658-77-3) – VP 0.08 Pa – Burnt sugar & jammy strawberry
* **Modifiers & Fixatives:** Ethyl Maltol, Sotolon, Vanillin, Tonka Bean Absolute / Coumarin
* **Olfactory Sensation:** Rich, dark, sticky preserve paste, sun-dried persimmons, and caramelized cooked jam.

### Slide 12: Master Parameter Matrix & Volatility Waterfall Model
* **Evaporative Waterfall Concept:**
  - High Volatility (>100 Pa): Hexanal, Ethyl Butanoate, cis-3-Hexenol (Top Note Diffusion)
  - Medium Volatility (1-100 Pa): IBMP, Stemone, Benzaldehyde (Heart Note Transition)
  - Substantive Low Volatility (<1 Pa): $\gamma$-Decalactone, Calone 1951, $\beta$-Damascenone (Drydown Tenacity)
* **Formulator Rule:** Omitting green pith (Facet 2) or lactones/seeds (Facets 5, 7) creates artificial candy. Multi-facet layering ensures natural living realism.
