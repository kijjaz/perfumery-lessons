THE BOTANICAL & LIFECYCLE FACET PARADIGM
Biological & Sensory Map

Deconstructing Fruits: Biology vs. Smell & Taste Perception

Synthetic fruit accords often smell like flat candy because perfumers rely solely on ester molecules (e.g. Ethyl Butanoate + Isoamyl Acetate). In nature, a living fruit is a complex biological organ composed of distinct anatomical tissues and lifecycle transformations.

🎛️ Student Flexibility Principle:

"The 10-Facet Map is an Olfactory Equalizer, not a set recipe. You choose which channels to unmute, adjust the faders, and substitute materials based on what is in your organ."

PLANT BIOCHEMISTRY & BIOSYNTHETIC PATHWAYS
Plant Biochemistry

The 5 Primary Enzymatic Engines of Fruit Volatiles

Living fruit aroma is not random; it is synthesized by 5 specific plant enzymatic cascades localized within distinct tissue compartments.

1. Lipoxygenase (LOX) Pathway (F1, F4, F8)

Polyunsaturated fatty acids (Linoleic 18:2 & Linolenic 18:3) cleaved by LOX/HPL into green C6 aldehydes (Hexanal, (E)-2-Hexenal 4GA02582), ADH-reduced to cis-3-Hexenol (F4), and AAT-esterified to cis-3-Hexenyl Acetate (F8).

2. BCAA Transamination (F3, F9)

BCAT aminotransferases convert L-Isoleucine, L-Leucine, and L-Valine into acyl-CoA precursors, forming ripe pulp esters (Ethyl 2-MB F3) and yeast fermentation esters (Isoamyl Acetate F9).

3. Shikimate / PAL Pathway (F3, F7)

L-Phenylalanine converted via PAL into cyanic pit stone drivers (Benzaldehyde F7) and balsamic esters (Benzyl Acetate, Methyl Cinnamate).

4. MEP / Terpenoid Pathway (F1, F5, F6)

Plastidial GPP synthases generate skin monoterpenes (d-Limonene, Myrcene, Terpinolene) and cyclic ethers (cis-Rose Oxide F5).

5. Carotenoid Cleavage (CCD) & Maillard Reaction (F10)

CCD Dioxygenases cleave beta-carotene into ultra-potent norisoprenoids (beta-Ionone 7 ppt, (E)-beta-Damascenone 0.002 ppb). Post-harvest dehydration caramelizes sugar-amino matrices into Furaneol & Sotolon.

TOPOLOGICAL LIFECYCLE FLOW & EVAPORATIVE TIMING
Topology & Kinetics

Spatial Tissue Topology vs. Temporal Evaporative Kinetics

"Topological" refers to spatial tissue adjacency (F1 Exocarp to F7 Seed) and non-linear post-harvest degradation vectors. Evaporative timing on skin is dictated by pure substance Vapor Pressure (VP in Pa at 25°C).

Phase 1: Top-Note Flash (0-15m)

High VP (>150 Pa): F1 Skin (Hexanal 1500 Pa), F4 Water (cis-3-Hexenol 200 Pa), F8 Calyx (Leaf Acetate 180 Pa), F9 Ferment (Ethyl Acetate 9700 Pa).

Instant green skin friction, juicy top lift, & boozy sparkle.

Phase 2: Heart Body (15m-2h)

Medium VP (1-150 Pa): F3 Pulp (Ethyl 2-MB 320 Pa), F6 Sap (Triplal 90 Pa), F5 Aril (3M3SBA 18 Pa), F7 Seed (Benzaldehyde 127 Pa).

Bridges crisp top greens into rich pulp & cyanic seed notes.

Phase 3: Base Drydown (2-24h+)

Low VP (<1 Pa): F3 Flesh (gamma-Decalactone 0.12 Pa), F10 Preserves (beta-Damascenone 0.25 Pa, Sotolon 0.02 Pa).

Sustains velvety peach fuzz & dark persimmon paste for hours.

PHYSICAL-CHEMICAL VOLATILITY WATERFALL MODEL
Evaporative Kinetics

10-Facet Evaporative Waterfall Partitioning on Skin

A living fruit does not evaporate in rigid steps. Layering raw materials across 3 Volatility Tiers creates a continuous evaporative cascade, preventing the "hollow gap" collapse between top notes and base drydown.

1. Diffusive Top Cascade

VP > 100 Pa | 0–30m
Target Facets:

Facet 1 (Skin), Facet 4 (Dew), Facet 8 (Calyx), Facet 9 (Ferment)

Key Materials:

Hexanal, Ethyl Butanoate, cis-3-Hexenol, Ethyl Acetate

Mechanism:

High VP molecules flash off alcohol, providing immediate green skin friction, juicy fruit lift, and dewy spark.

2. Structural Heart Transition

VP 1–100 Pa | 30m–2h
Target Facets:

Facet 2 (Pith), Facet 5 (Aril), Facet 6 (Sap), Facet 7 (Seed)

Key Materials:

IBMP, Stemone, Benzaldehyde, 3M3SBA, 2,3,5-TMP

Mechanism:

Intermediate actors prevent formula fracturing. Bitter pyrazines and cyanic pit notes bridge top greens into heavy fruit pulp.

3. Substantive Base Drydown

VP < 1 Pa | 2–8+ hrs
Target Facets:

Facet 3 (Pulp), Facet 10 (Desiccated Preserves)

Key Materials:

γ-Decalactone, β-Damascenone, Calone 1951, Furaneol

Mechanism:

Low VP lactones bind skin lipids, sustaining velvety peach fuzz, jammy preserves, and humid sillage long after top notes fade.
