4D Life Cycle: The 3 Temporal Evaluation Windows
Evaporation Kinetics & Perceptual Trajectory
Window 1: The Flash
0 to 15 Minutes
Blinding peppery-citrus projection: A massive wall of Bergapten-free Bergamot Oil (14.5% – 25%) charged with Dihydromyrcenol, tart Methyl Pamplemousse, sharp Citral (8%), Pink Pepper, and trace aldehydes C10/C11. Strikes the air with unmistakable modern electric radiance.
Window 2: The Bloom
30 Minutes to 2 Hours
As the flash settles, Hedione (10% – 40%) expands the heart into warm air, carrying French Lavender Oil, green Geranium, and resinous Elemi. The signature tingling vibration of Szechuan Pepper CO2 provides a dry spicy bite while trace Damascone Alpha and Helional keep the floralcy dewy.
Window 3: The Drydown
4 Hours to 24+ Hours
The nuclear mineral drydown emerges: An unprecedented overdose of Ambroxan / Cetalox (up to 20%) anchored by Iso E Super / Timbersilk (13% – 35%), Fractionated Patchouli / Clearwood, clean laundry musks, dry Vertofix, and smoky Vetiver SFE. Ambrocenide provides persistent multi-day radiance.
Architectural Anatomy & Thematic Accords
The Neo-Fougère Amber Paradigm

1. The Monolithic Ambroxan Overdose

Prior to Sauvage, ambroxan was deployed in delicate fractions (0.5%–2%) as an expensive ambergris simulator. Demachy pushed it to an audacious 5% to 20% overdose, creating a hyper-modern mineral, clean-sweat, warm-pebble radiance that completely redefined 2010s masculine sillage.

Ambroxan serves not just as a fixative, but as the central protagonist vibrating from opening flash to late drydown.

2. The Pepper-Citrus Shock Wave

The top is not a traditional gentleman's cologne opening; it is a high-velocity shock driven by peppery bergamot combined with Szechuan Pepper CO2 (which contains sanshool, inducing a slight numbing vibration on olfactory receptors) and terpene-rich pink pepper.

This spicy bite cuts through the massive amber base, preventing any heavy cloying dullness.

3. The Transparent Superstructure

Nearly 50% of Sauvage's chassis is pure transparent volume: Hedione (up to 40%) plus Iso E Super / Timbersilk (up to 35%).

This vast, weightless, velvety cloud allows high concentrations of dense naturals (bergamot, lavender, patchouli, vetiver) to project aggressively with a luminous "shower-gel fresh" aura.

Compounding & Analytical Chemistry Insights
GCMS Calibrations & Organ Practicalities

1. The Ambroxan Solubility Threshold: Compounding Ambroxan at 5% to 20% requires high solvent capacity from the formula itself. In Sauvage, the massive quantities of Iso E Super (up to 35%), Hedione (up to 40%), and Dihydromyrcenol act as the primary co-solvents, keeping crystalline Ambroxan fully dissolved without recrystallizing in high-proof ethanol.


2. Bergapten-Free (FCF) Citrus Requirement: Standard cold-pressed bergamot oil contains furocoumarins (principally bergapten) which are strictly phototoxic and regulated by IFRA Category 4 limits (< 15 ppm bergapten). The massive 14.5%–25% bergamot dosage in Sauvage is only feasible through molecularly distilled, furocoumarin-free (FCF) qualities.


3. Sanshool & The Pepper Spark Illusion: Szechuan pepper (Zanthoxylum) contains hydroxy-alpha-sanshool, an alkylamide that activates somatosensory receptors (TRPV1 and TRPA1), producing a physiological tingling or light numbing vibration on the nasal mucosa. Paired with pink pepper and citral, this creates an active physical "hum" in the top flash that synthetic citrus alone cannot mimic.


4. Clearwood vs Traditional Patchouli: While classic fougères use crude patchouli, Sauvage relies heavily on fractionated patchouli MD or Clearwood (fermentation-derived patchoulol). This removes dark earthy terpenes, dirty camphor, and heavy color, delivering crystalline woody tenacity that leaves the crisp shower-gel aesthetic pristine.
