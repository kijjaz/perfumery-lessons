Lesson 2.1: The Volatility Curve & Functional Roles

Module 2: The Functional Hierarchy (Physics for Artists)
Lesson Overview

Difficulty: Intermediate | Estimated Time: 90 Minutes

Master the physics of perfume evaporation governed by Vapor Pressure (Pv), Molecular Weight (MW), and Odor Detection Thresholds. Map materials into the Architectural Casting System.

1. The Architectural Casting System

Role Casting Metaphor Vapor Pressure (Pv) Mechanical Purpose Examples
The Heart The Protagonist 0.01 - 0.1 mmHg Establishes core identity and volume. Hedione, PEA, Geraniol
The Modifier The Lighting / FX > 0.5 mmHg Distorts the core, forces instant projection. Sweet Orange Oil, Aldehydes
The Blender The Stage Manager Intermediate Unifies blend, prevents formula fracturing. Linalool, Linalyl Acetate
The Fixative Gravity < 0.001 mmHg Anchors volatile notes to skin. Velvione, Galaxolide, Ambroxan
