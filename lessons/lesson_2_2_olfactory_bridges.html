Lesson 2.2: The Art of the Olfactory Bridge

Module 2: The Functional Hierarchy (Physics for Artists)
Lesson Overview

Difficulty: Intermediate | Estimated Time: 90 Minutes

Understand why fragrances fracture, contrast horizontal Blenders with vertical Bridges, and master monoterpene alcohols, aldehydic blenders, and synthetic woods.

1. Blender vs. Bridge Architecture

The Blender (Horizontal)

Operates across materials in the same evaporation phase. Dilutes sharp edges and smooths out clashing notes within a single tier.

The Bridge (Vertical)

Operates vertically across evaporation gaps. Possesses olfactory facets of both volatile top notes and heavy base anchors to prevent formula fracturing.
