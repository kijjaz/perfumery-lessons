Lesson 1.1: The Language of Scent & Olfactory Training

Phase 1: Olfactory Foundation & Scent Vocabulary
Lesson Overview

Difficulty: Beginner | Estimated Time: 90 Minutes

Master standard smelling techniques using mouillettes without olfactory fatigue, differentiate closely related synthetic and natural compounds, and construct a structured Visual Scent Vocabulary.

1. Core Theory: The Olfactory Pathway & Vocabulary

1.1 The Olfactory Mechanism

When volatile molecules travel into nasal passages, they dissolve in the olfactory mucous layer and bind to Olfactory Receptor Neurons (ORNs). These send signals directly to the olfactory bulb, bypassing the thalamus into the limbic system (emotional/memory center).

1.2 Moving Beyond Subjective Language

Abandon non-descriptive terms like "nice" or "perfumey". Use a structured Olfactory Vocabulary:

  • Olfactory Families: Citrus, Floral, Woody, Green, Spicy, Amber/Balsamic, Musk, Fruity, Animalic.
  • Sensory Descriptors: Zesty, powdery, herbaceous, jammy, waxy, metallic, camphorous, dry, indolic, phenolic.
  • Texture & Weight: Sharp, smooth, heavy, airy, velvet, chalky, thick, transparent.

2. Material Spotlight (RDKit Vector Structures)

Linalool 78-70-6

Fresh, clean, woody-floral, strongly reminiscent of lavender and coriander seeds. Transparent and airy top/heart note blender.

Linalyl Acetate 115-95-7

Clean, citrus-like, sweet, bergamot-like ester. Principal ester found in natural Lavender and Bergamot oils.
