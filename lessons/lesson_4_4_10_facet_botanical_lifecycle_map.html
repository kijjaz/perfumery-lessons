4. Formulator Insights & Epistemic Tagging

Peer-Reviewed Fact

1. Multi-Facet Sub-Type Synergism in Fruit Accord Reconstruction

Molecular sensory science and GC-O omission studies prove that recreating a natural fruit profile requires materials targeting both high-volatility top-notes (Facets 1, 3, 4) and low-volatility structural anchors (Facets 2, 6, 7, 10). Reconstitution blends omitting green pith pyrazines (Facet 2) or lactonic aril/seed carriers (Facets 5, 7) are consistently perceived as synthetic "candy-like" ester mixtures rather than natural living fruits. (Ref: J. Agric. Food Chem., 2016; Food Chem., 2021)

Theoretical Hypothesis / Formulator Heuristic

2. Aqueous-Dewy Vapor Pressure Partitioning & Skin Substantivity Waterfall Model

Combining ultra-high volatility C6 aldehydes (Hexanal, VP = 1200 Pa) with medium-volatility dewy ketones (Melonal, VP = 140 Pa) and substantive lactones (γ-Decalactone, VP = 0.12 Pa) creates an evaporative "waterfall gradient" on skin. The hypothesis that high-water-phase materials like Calone 1951 (logP = 1.80) alter the thermodynamic activity coefficient of lipophilic esters in hydroalcoholic sprays to project a "humid spray cloud" sillage without suppressing heart-note tenacity remains a theoretical hypothesis requiring dynamic headspace volatility testing.
