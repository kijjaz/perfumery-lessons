Pyrazines in Tea & Perfumery

Dilute to 0.1–1%

Nitrogen heterocycles generated during roasting. Perfumers mix real tea pyrazines with synthetic organ tools to control toastiness.

🌿 Natural 2-Acetyl Pyrazine: Popcorn & roasted barley in Genmaicha/Hojicha.
🧪 Perfumer Synth Pyralone (IBQ) & Filbertone: Leather-green astringency and roasted nut facets.
🪵

Trimethoxybenzene & Relatives

Vintage Earth

1,2,3-Trimethoxybenzene is a natural marker of microbial Pu-erh. Perfumers replicate it using close organ relatives.

🌿 Natural Veratrole (1,2-Dimethoxybenzene): Antique wood & dry cedar found in natural Pu-erh headspace.
🧪 Perfumer Synth Clearwood & Evernyl: Biotech patchouli heart & oakmoss synth for clean damp cellar notes.
🍎

Damascenone & Organ Substitutes

IFRA Facet Boosters

Natural β-Damascenone gives black tea its jammy stewed-apple note, but has strict IFRA restrictions. Perfumers use synthetic organ relatives.

🌿 Natural β-Damascenone: Ultra-potent natural norisoprenoid in black tea (0.002 ppb threshold).
🧪 Perfumer Synth Givescone & Ethyl Safranate: Synthetic materials providing damascone fruit-rose-saffron facets without IFRA limit issues.

Hedione & The Jasmonates

Radiant Air, Dewy Steam & Floral Humidity
30–50% of Tea Base

Hedione (Methyl Dihydrojasmonate) is the undisputed invisible canvas of tea perfumery (pioneered by Jean-Claude Ellena in Bvlgari’s Eau de Thé Vert). It creates a transparent, radiant, vaporous lift that mimics hot tea steam without adding heavy, sweet floral weight.

cis-Jasmone (Use diluted at 10%) 🌿 Natural in Tea

The natural oxylipin found in bruised tea leaves. Acts as Hedione's booster—adding warm, green, waxy petal texture and authentic tea-leaf radiance.

Methyl Jasmonate (Less common / Natural) 🌿 Natural in Tea

The natural parent ester directly in fresh tea leaves & jasmine petals. Higher cost than synthetic Hedione, but imparts an uncommonly rich, humid, live-flower tea depth.

💡 Perfumer Rule: Build your tea base around 30-40% Hedione + 0.3-0.5% cis-Jasmone (10%) to instantly transform flat green molecules into floating tea steam!
🪵

Iso E Super & The Ionone Link

The Woody-Powdery Velvet Spine
0–35% (Optional)

Tea leaves naturally release β-Ionone & α-Ionone during withering via carotenoid breakdown. Ionones supply the signature powdery-violet, soft dried-apricot, and dry woody facets essential to white, green, and black tea.

The Iso E Super Chemical Synergy (Optional: 0-35%) 🧪 Perfumer Synth

Iso E Super provides a smooth, transparent, velvety cedarwood-amber texture. It can be omitted (0%) for pure, crystalline tea bases, or used up to 35% to lock the dry, powdery violet-wood facets of β-Ionone onto a subtle cedar skeleton.

Fixing Volatile Top Notes to Skin ✨ 3D Volume

Green top notes like cis-3-hexenol evaporate rapidly. When included, Iso E Super + Ionones catch these fugitive green notes, grounding them into a persistent, cozy, suede-like drydown.

💡 Perfumer Blueprint: Combining Hedione + (0–35% Iso E Super) + β-Ionone creates the flexible 3D "floral-woody tea base matrix" used across niche tea formulations.

The 4 Scent-Creation Pathways

Where tea molecules originate
🌿

1. Lipid & Oxylipin Pathway

Lipoxygenase (LOX) Cascades

Breaks leaf lipids into two key facets:
• HPL Branch: Snapped C6 leaf greens.
• AOS Branch: Jasmonates synthesized during leaf bruising and stress.

Key Molecules:
Greens: cis-3-hexenol, Hexanal, cis-3-hexenyl acetate
Jasmonates: cis-Jasmone, Methyl Jasmonate
🧪 Synth: Hedione
🍑

2. Violet & Fruit Magic

Carotenoid Cleavage

Sunlight and oxidation chop leaf carotenoid pigments into rich C13 norisoprenoids supplying velvety dried-fruit and powdery violet depth.

Key Molecules: β-Ionone, β-Damascenone, Theaspiranes
🌹

3. Unlocked Floral Gems

Glycoside Hydrolysis

Endogenous glycosidase enzymes break sugar bonds, liberating trapped volatile floral terpenes and alcohols into fragrant air.

Key Molecules: Linalool, Geraniol, Phenyl Ethyl Alcohol (PEA), Methyl Salicylate
🔥

4. Toast & Microbial Aging

Maillard Pyrolysis & Microbes

Thermal Heat: High roasting forms toasted pyrazines & furans.
Microbial Aging: Fungi convert polyphenols into trimethoxybenzenes & linalool oxides.

Key Molecules: 2-Acetyl Pyrazine, 1,2,3-Trimethoxybenzene, Veratrole, Linalool Oxides

🎨 The Perfumer's Takeaway

The spectrum of tea in perfumery is endless. By balancing Bergamot FCF, Leaf Alcohol (cis-3-hexenol), Floral Hedione, Beta-Ionone, and Rose Alcohols (PEA / Mefrosol / Geraniol) with trace pyrazines or wood ethers (veratrole/trimethoxybenzene), you can build any tea base from a misty Sencha to a dark Assam or aged Pu-erh.
