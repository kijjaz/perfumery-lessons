Minimalist Synergies & The Ellena Framework
Lesson Overview

Difficulty: Beginner/Intermediate | Estimated Time: 90 Minutes

Understand why the traditional Olfactory Pyramid fails, master the Ellena Framework (Classicals, Baroques, Minimalists, Abstracts), and construct Olfactory Haikus using 2-part synthetic synergies.

1. Core Theory & The Olfactory Haiku

1.1 The Olfactory Haiku Paradigm

An Olfactory Haiku is a formula of only 2 to 3 molecules that interact to form a vivid, figurative illusion (e.g. green tea, bitter grapefruit, peach skin) using negative space.

1.2 The Trace Natural Bridge (<1% Dosing)

Synthetic Haikus are architecturally sharp, but can sometimes feel sterile or 2-dimensional. Adding a micro-trace of a natural essential oil like Ylang Ylang Extra EO (0.1% to 0.5%) introduces hundreds of organic trace ester & sesquiterpene impurities. At <1%, it does NOT turn the blend heavy floral; instead, it rounds off synthetic edges and breathes living botanical warmth into the minimalist skeleton.
