THE DEATH OF THE OLFACTORY PYRAMID
Core Concept

Physics vs. Marketing Fiction

The 3-tier pyramid (Top/Middle/Base) was created by 19th-century perfumers when natural oils dominated. Modern high-impact synthetics (Hedione, Iso E Super, Ambroxan) evaporate continuously across all stages, rendering the rigid pyramid obsolete.

THE ELLENA FRAMEWORK: 4 AESTHETIC ARCHETYPES
Core Concept

Classifying Fragrance Topology

Jean-Claude Ellena classifies fragrances into 4 structural geometries: 1. CLASSICALS: Balanced, symmetrical archetypal geometry (e.g. Traditional Eau de Cologne). 2. BAROQUES: Extreme spatial volume, tension, and contrast (e.g. Overdosed gourmand/ambers). 3. MINIMALISTS: Transparent aura, short formula, high-purity illusions (e.g. Terre d'Hermès). 4. ABSTRACTS: Synthetic block geometry with no direct botanical reference (e.g. Chanel No. 5).
