THE 4-PART ARCHITECTURAL CASTING SYSTEM
Architectural Casting

Structural Engineering Disguised as Art

Every ingredient in a formula fulfills a distinct mechanical role based on physical chemistry: 1. THE BASIC (HEART): Moderate VP, high threshold. Establishes structural mass & identity. 2. THE MODIFIER / DIFFUSER: High VP, high volatility. Forces projection & draws attention. 3. THE BLENDER / BRIDGE: Intersecting boiling points. Structural glue preventing gaps. 4. THE FIXATIVE: Extremely low VP, high MW. Anchors volatile elements to skin.
