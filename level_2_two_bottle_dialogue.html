2 Your Nose is the Only Judge

Weigh out these parts into a small clean vial (e.g. 0.50g of each on an analytical scale), swirl gently, and dip a fresh paper smelling strip.

*Notice that we do NOT tell you what this smells like or which balance is "correct". Perfumery is your personal sensory dialogue.*

Questions to ask your nose:

  • Can you still pick out Bottle A and Bottle B individually, or did they merge into a completely new third identity (an accord)?
  • Which of the two speaks first when you wave the strip in front of your nose?
  • Smell the strip again in 30 minutes. Has the conversation shifted? Who is still talking?
