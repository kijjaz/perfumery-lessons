Warm Almond & Sweet Hay

Coumarin (Tonka Bean)

The creamy heart of sweet cut hay, toasted marzipan almond, and warm tobacco leaves.

Why Compose Coumarin from Raw Materials?

1. The Tonka Bean Precipitation Trap

Natural Tonka Bean absolute is packed with dark waxy fats and coumarin crystals that precipitate out of cold alcohol, clogging bottle sprayers and leaving dark brown stains on white clothing.

2. Synthetic Crystals = Clean Shaving Soap

Pure synthetic Coumarin crystals provide the pristine, powdery almond-hay aroma without the heavy, sticky brown resin, allowing perfumers to create crisp, clean barbershop foam.

3. Strict IFRA 51st Compliance

Coumarin is restricted to ~1.5% in finished Category 4 fine fragrances. Natural tonka varies wildly in coumarin content, whereas synthetic crystals guarantee mathematically precise safety audits.

4. The 1882 Birth of Synthetic Perfumery

Synthesized in 1868, Paul Parquet used it in *Fougère Royale (1882)* to invent the first abstract fantasy perfume family, freeing perfumery from merely copying natural flower waters!

The Multi-Facet Reality of Coumarin

Coumarin is not just "sweet tonka". It is a multifaceted agricultural and grooming prism:

Facet 1 (Dominant)
Toasted Sweet Almond

Warm marzipan, bitter almond pastry, and caramelized tonka beans.

Facet 2 (Agricultural)
Sun-Cured Sweetgrass Hay

Dry summer sweetgrass and golden hay bales baking under the afternoon sun.

Facet 3 (Grooming)
Clean Shaving Cream Foam

When paired with Lavender, creates the unmistakable clean soapy lather of a luxury shave.

Facet 4 (Warmth)
Cured Blonde Tobacco

Rich, aromatic sweet pipe tobacco leaves drying in wooden sheds.
