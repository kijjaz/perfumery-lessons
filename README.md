## 📚 Curriculum Breakdown & Interactive Web Apps

### 🟢 Module 1: The Olfactory Haiku & Lexicon of Form
Deconstructing traditional marketing pyramids, mastering the Ellena Framework, and formulating 2-part synthetic synergies using negative space.
* 📖 **[Lesson 1.1: Scent Language & Olfactory Training](https://kijjaz.github.io/perfumery-lessons/lessons/lesson_1_1_scent_language.html)** — Master mouillette techniques, ORN nasal pathways, and objective sensory descriptors.
* 🪶 **[Lesson 1.2: The Olfactory Haiku & Scent Aesthetics](https://kijjaz.github.io/perfumery-lessons/lessons/lesson_1_2_olfactory_haiku.html)** — Includes the interactive **Olfactory Haiku Formula Builder Widget**.

### 🟩 Module 2: The Functional Hierarchy (Physics for Artists)
Translating physical-chemical parameters—Vapor Pressure ($P_v$), Molecular Weight ($MW$), and Odor Detection Thresholds—into dynamic formula architecture.
* 📈 **[Lesson 2.1: The Volatility Curve & Functional Roles](https://kijjaz.github.io/perfumery-lessons/lessons/lesson_2_1_volatility_roles.html)** — The Architectural Casting System (Heart, Modifier, Blender, Fixative).
* 🌉 **[Lesson 2.2: The Art of the Olfactory Bridge](https://kijjaz.github.io/perfumery-lessons/lessons/lesson_2_2_olfactory_bridges.html)** — Mechanics of formula fracturing, horizontal blenders vs. vertical structural bridges.

### 🥭 Module 4: Spatial Volume & Trace Chemistry
Deconstructing natural fruit profiles into 3D botanical facets and engineering trace sulfur chemistry at picogram thresholds.
* ⚛️ **[Lesson 4.3: Trace Sulfur Engineering](https://kijjaz.github.io/perfumery-lessons/lessons/lesson_4_3_sulfur_dynamics.html)** — Scent engineering at picogram detection levels (3M3SBA, thiols, oximes).
* 🧊 **[Lesson 4.4: The 10-Facet Botanical & Lifecycle Map](https://kijjaz.github.io/perfumery-lessons/lessons/lesson_4_4_10_facet_botanical_lifecycle_map.html)** — 8 Anatomical/Spatial + 2 Temporal/Thermal transformation facets. Features the live **Evaporative Volatility Waterfall Calculator**.
* 📺 **[Presentation Deck: 10-Facet Fruit Architecture](https://kijjaz.github.io/perfumery-lessons/lessons/slides_lesson_4_4_10_facet_map.html)** — Fullscreen interactive 13-slide presentation deck with photorealistic AI imagery.

### 🍵 Supplementary Readings & Botanical Atlases
* 🍃 **[The Aroma Chemistry of Tea: An Olfactory Atlas](https://kijjaz.github.io/perfumery-lessons/lessons/tea_perfumery_aroma_chemistry_infographic.html)** — Comprehensive interactive atlas covering the 4 scent-creation chemical pathways of *Camellia sinensis* (lipid oxidation, carotenoid degradation, glycosides, and Maillard roasting) across 9 tea categories with perfumer reconstitution formulas.

## 🎨 Key Features

1. **Carbon & Gold Aesthetic System**: Styled with matte carbon dark modes (`#070708`), gold highlights (`#d4af37`), and glassmorphism cards.
2. **Chemically Accurate RDKit Vector SVGs**: All aroma chemicals are rendered in clean inline vector graphics.
3. **Interactive Bench Calculators**: Real-time JavaScript tools for dosing formulas, evaluating vapor pressure gradients, and simulating evaporation curves.
4. **Google Apps Script Generator**: Embedded standalone Google Apps Script (`create_google_slides.js`) to generate Google Slides presentations automatically.

## 🚀 GitHub Pages Deployment Setup

To host this course live on your own GitHub Pages:
1. Fork or clone this repository.
2. Go to **Settings -> Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, select:
   - **Source**: `Deploy from a branch`
   - **Branch**: `main` / `/ (root)`
4. Save. Your portal will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/perfumery-lessons/`.
