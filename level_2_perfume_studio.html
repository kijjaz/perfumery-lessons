3 Stage 5: Testing & Blotter Diagnostics

Evaluate the evaporation timeline on testing blotters before final bottling:

0 to 15 Minutes (Opening)

French Lavender and Bergamot create a sharp, invigorating herbal spark, instantly diffused by Hedione.

30 Min to 2 Hours (Heart)

Rose PEA bridges the herbal head to the coumarin base, creating the unmistakable clean lather of barbershop shaving cream.

4 to 8+ Hours (Drydown)

Coumarin and Evernyl lock with Iso E Super into a salty-sweet lichen forest floor that clings tenaciously to skin.
