2. The 4 Functional Roles (Formulation by Function)

Every material in a formula must serve a specific architectural purpose. We don't just mix "good smells"; we engineer by function:

Role 1
Heart (Core Theme)

The main protagonist and recognizable character of the block or perfume. The central story pillar.

e.g. The juicy fig pulp; the dewy rose petals
Role 2
Modifier & Diffuser

Adds nuance, contrast, and radiant projection. Gives edge to the heart and lifts it into the air so it isn't flat.

e.g. Stemone (crisp edge), Hedione (dewy lift)
Role 3
Blender & Bridge

Connects contrasting notes, melting them together to prevent harsh seams or awkward gaps in the structure.

e.g. Linalool (citrus to wood), Amyl Salicylate
Role 4
Fixative & Anchor

Bulky, slow-evaporating molecules that anchor the blend, prolonging its lifespan on skin without smothering diffusion.

e.g. Ambroxan, Iso E Super, Ethylene Brassylate

Tier 2 • Historical Blueprints
🏛️ Fragrance Archetypes

Landmark historical formula skeletons created through synthetic chemical breakthroughs.

1709 • Giovanni Maria Farina
Eau de Cologne Chassis
Bergamot + Lemon + Neroli + Rosemary
Volatile, restorative citrus splash. Short-lived (30m) designed for hygienic refreshment.
Modern Twist: Anchor with 40% Iso E Super & Hedione for all-day transparent sillage.
1882 • Paul Parquet (Houbigant)
Fougère Royale Chassis
Lavender + Geranium + Coumarin + Oakmoss
Birth of modern synthetic perfumery. The paradoxical barbershop tension between fresh herbal top and sweet hay-moss base.
Modern Twist: Replace banned atranorin oakmoss with Evernyl + aerated Florol/Hedione wash.
1917 • François Coty
Chypre de Coty Chassis
Bergamot + Rose/Jasmine + Labdanum + Oakmoss/Patchouli
The monumental chiaroscuro of scent: solar citrus light contrasting against damp, dark mossy shadow.
Modern Twist: Neo-Chypre using Clearwood® + IBQ + pink pepper and transparent Florol.
1925 • Jacques Guerlain (Shalimar)
Classical Amber / Balsamic
Ethyl Vanillin (Overdose) + Labdanum + Bergamot
Rich, golden, resinous cocoon. Replaces outdated Eurocentric label 'Oriental' with standard Amber taxonomy.
Modern Twist: Woody-Amber mineral drydown using Ambroxan, Cedramber, and modern Cashmeran.
1992 • Olivier Cresp (Thierry Mugler Angel)
Modern Gourmand Chassis
Ethyl Maltol (Caramel) + Patchouli (30%) + Hedione
The shock of confectionary sugar anchored by dirty, earthy patchouli camphoric darkness.
Modern Twist: Modern lactonic sandalwood with subtle roasted tonka & salted caramel facets.
Archetypes serve as structural blueprints during Mind-mapping and Formulation.

🎓 SFP (7 Families) vs. Edwards Wheel:
• The Professional Standard (SFP): Recognizes 7 sovereign families: Citrus (Hespéridée), Floral, Fougère, Chypre, Woody, Amber, and Leather.
• The Retail Wheel (Michael Edwards): Designed for sales floors with 4 consumer quadrants. Because Fougère spans fresh citrus, herbal/floral lavender, ambery coumarin, and woody oakmoss, Edwards placed it in the center hub as a cross-family bridge.
• The Lavender Reality: While botanically a flower, perfumers formulate lavender for its aromatic, camphoraceous, and fresh herbal facets—not as a soft petal floral. At the bench, formulation is guided by chemistry and functional roles, not marketing charts!

How each taxonomy tier is operationalized at each step of the commercial development workflow:

Stage 5 Evaluation Protocol: The 3 Temporal Windows
Window 1 • 0 to 15 Minutes
The Initial Volatile Flash

Dominated by high vapor pressure molecules (Citrus peel, Cis-3-Hexenol, Aldehydes). Evaluates first impression and initial spray opening without nasal burnout.

Window 2 • 30 Min to 2 Hours
The Heart Transition (Coeur)

Medium volatility blooms (Hedione, Florol, Rose PEA, Lavender). Evaluates the true personality, sillage trail, and harmonic balance of the main floral/aromatic accords.

Window 3 • 4 to 12+ Hours
The Balsamic Drydown

Low vapor pressure anchors (Iso E Super, Evernyl, Labdanum, Vanillin, Musks). Evaluates skin tenacity, fabric substantivity, and smooth fadeout without sour residual notes.
