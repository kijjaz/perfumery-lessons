Universal Diffuser & Amplifier

Hedione (Methyl Dihydrojasmonate)

The acoustic amplifier of modern perfumery: transparent floral wind that makes perfumes project and breathe.

What Does Hedione Bring to the Table?

1. The "Acoustic Amplifier"

On its own, Hedione smells very faint—like wet dewy lemon water. But when added to other materials, it acts like a loudspeaker, blowing wind through flowers and woods so they radiate across a room.

2. Creates "Negative Space"

Heavy natural oils crowd together and suffocating the nose. Hedione spreads molecules apart, creating airy "breathing room" (like white space in graphic design).

3. The Modern Jasmine Secret

Discovered by Firmenich in 1962 while analyzing natural Jasmine absolute, master perfumer Edmond Roudnitska used it in *Eau Sauvage (1966)* to invent transparent modern perfumery.

4. Green Tea & Dewy Petals

When combined with Level 1's Citrus and Green Leaf, Hedione forms the world's most famous green tea accord (Bvlgari *Thé Vert*).

The Cis-Isomer Secret: Hedione vs. Hedione HC

When Firmenich chemist Edouard Demole synthesized Hedione in 1962, he discovered it was a mixture of stereoisomers. But only one isomer carries the radiant magic!

1. Standard Hedione (~10% Cis)

Standard Hedione is ~90% trans (which has almost no odor) and only ~10% cis. It is cheap, transparent, and used in high percentages (15% - 40%) as a sheer atmospheric background wash.

2. Hedione HC (High Cis: 75%+ Cis)

Hedione HC (High Cis) concentrates the active stereoisomer. It is over 5 times more powerful, with an intense, blooming jasmine-petal radiance, green tea crispness, and electric projection!

The Multi-Facet Reality of Hedione

Hedione is not just "watery jasmine". It is a multi-dimensional atmospheric lens:

Facet 1 (Dominant)
Dewy White Petals

Sheer, weightless jasmine petals freshly washed by rain.

Facet 2 (Citrus-Tea)
Green Tea & Watery Lemon

Crisp, cooling iced tea aroma that blends effortlessly with Bergamot.

Facet 3 (Acoustic)
Negative Space & Diffusion

Acts like sound waves, pushing flowers and woods outward across a room.

Facet 4 (Modifier)
Cool Morning Air

A clean, ozonic breeze that prevents perfumes from feeling stuffy.
