Florol (Floralpyran)

The secret weapon of modern floral transparency: fresh white lily-of-the-valley petals drenched in cool morning dew.

Why Compose with Florol? (The Hydroxycitronellal Successor)

1. Replacing Hydroxycitronellal

For decades, the classic dewy lily-of-the-valley was built on Hydroxycitronellal. While not banned, Hydroxycitronellal is heavily restricted by IFRA (~1% max in Cat 4) and prone to oxidation/Schiff base formation. Florol replaced it as an ultra-stable, skin-safe, unrestricted alternative!

2. Superior Chemical Stability

Unlike aldehydes which oxidize into harsh acids over time, Florol is a pyran ether-alcohol: chemically indestructible, resistant to discoloration, and stable in high heat and alkaline bases.

3. Softening Leather into Beige Suede

When combined with Isobutyl Quinoline (IBQ), Florol's creamy, dewy petal texture takes the aggressive, sharp edge off raw leather hide, buffering it into soft, luxurious suede.

4. The Neo-Chypre Heart

Classic 1917 Chypres relied on dense vintage florals. Modern Neo-Chypres (*Coco Mademoiselle*) use Florol to give the floral core a weightless, glowing petal radiance.

The Multi-Facet Reality of Florol

Florol is not just a single lily note. It is an indispensable modern petal prism:

Facet 1 (Dominant)
Dewy White Muguet

Crisp, watery lily-of-the-valley petals drenched in cool morning mist.

Facet 2 (Freshness)
Watery Melon / Cucumber

Subtle aquatic green rind that adds transparent hydration to floral accords.

Facet 3 (Body)
Creamy Petal Substance

Velvety floral body that adds tactile weight without adding powder.

Facet 4 (Modifier)
Clean Textile Freshness

Smooth, newly laundered white linen finish that modernizes base notes.
