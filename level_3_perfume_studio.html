3 Stage 5: Testing & Blotter Diagnostics

Audit the dramatic contrast between light and dark across the evaporation curve before final bottling:

0 to 15 Minutes (Opening)

Blinding flash of Italian Bergamot FCF, revealing hints of bitter quinoline leather beneath.

30 Min to 2 Hours (Heart)

Florol and Rose provide a dewy white-and-pink petal bridge, keeping the fragrance elegant and floral.

4 to 12+ Hours (Drydown)

Deep balsamic Labdanum, earthy Clearwood, and salty Evernyl form an indestructible, legendary base trail.
